IT Request Escalation Policy

Section 1: Standard Escalation Triggers
1. If an approver is unavailable for more than 2 business days, the request is escalated to the next approval level.
2. If a request has been formally rejected twice, it is escalated to the department head for a final decision.
3. High-priority requests must be reviewed within 24 hours; if not actioned within 24 hours, they are automatically escalated regardless of approver availability.
4. Requests above 10000 USD are escalated to the director level even if the assigned approver is available.

Section 2: Multi-Level and Compound Escalation
5. Infrastructure requests above 5000 USD with high or critical priority are escalated to both the senior manager and the finance team simultaneously, not sequentially.
6. If an approver is unavailable AND the request amount exceeds 10000 USD, the request is escalated directly to the director, skipping all intermediate approval levels.

Section 3: Fast-Track Escalation
7. Requests submitted by employees at Level 5 or above are fast-tracked: the review window is reduced to 12 hours, and the first escalation goes directly to the senior manager rather than the standard next level.
8. Critical-priority requests that qualify for the infrastructure emergency exception (Rejection Policy Section 3) are processed on a fast-track basis even while documentation is pending.

Section 4: Escalation vs Rejection Precedence
9. Rejection conditions defined in the Rejection Policy take precedence over escalation triggers. The sole exception is critical-priority infrastructure requests as defined in Rejection Policy Section 3, which are escalated rather than rejected.
