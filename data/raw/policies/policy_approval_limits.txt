IT Approval policy - Transaction limits

1.Any request below 1000 USD can be auto-approved.
2. Requests between 1000 USD and 5000 USD require manager approval.
3. Requests above 5000 USD require senior manager and finance team approval.
4. Requests above 10000 USD must be escalated to the director level.
5. All approvals must be completed within 3 business days.
