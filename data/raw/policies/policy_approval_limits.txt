IT Request Approval Policy — Transaction Limits

Section 1: Standard Approval Tiers
1. Software license and training requests under 1000 USD are auto-approved if documentation is complete and the account is active or under review.
2. All other request types (hardware, infrastructure, travel) under 500 USD are auto-approved under the same conditions.
3. Requests between 1000 USD and 5000 USD require manager approval.
4. Requests between 5000 USD and 10000 USD require both senior manager and finance team approval.
5. Requests above 10000 USD must be escalated to the director level regardless of approver availability.
6. All approvals must be completed within 3 business days.

Section 2: Request-Type Specific Overrides
7. Infrastructure requests above 2000 USD require finance sign-off in addition to the standard approval tier, regardless of priority level.
8. Travel requests above 3000 USD require an additional pre-approved justification document to be attached before the request can proceed to any approval stage.

Section 3: Annual Budget Caps by Request Type
9. Training requests are subject to a maximum per-request cap of 5000 USD. Requests exceeding this cap are treated as budget violations under Rejection Policy Section 1 and are immediately rejected.
10. Travel requests are subject to a maximum per-request cap of 8000 USD. Requests exceeding this cap are treated as budget violations and are immediately rejected.

Section 4: Emergency Exception
11. Critical-priority infrastructure requests up to 5000 USD may be fast-tracked with manager approval only, bypassing the standard senior manager and finance requirement. A waiver documenting the emergency justification must be filed within 48 hours of approval.
